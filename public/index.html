Concrete Calculator · formula

volume V = length × width × thickness

order V × (1 + waste ÷ 100)

bags ⌈ order ÷ yield per bag ⌉

example 10 × 10 × 0.333 ft = 33.33 ft³

+ 10 % = 36.67 ft³ = 1.36 yd³

36.67 ÷ 0.60 ft³ = 61.1 → 62 bags

Questions

The ones people ask before they order materials.

Read the guides

How accurate is each calculator?

The maths is exact. The result is only as good as your measurements and the bag yield or density you enter, so measure twice.

How much extra should I order?

Each calculator suggests an allowance: 5 to 10 % for concrete, about 10 % for straight tile, 15 % for showers and diagonal layouts.

How many 80 lb bags make a cubic yard?

About 45, at the common yield of 0.60 ft³ a bag. Check the yield printed on your product.

Can a calculator size my footing or slab?

No. It works out quantities. Thickness and reinforcement come from your plans, an engineer and your local building code.

Is it free, and is my data stored?

It is free and needs no account. Calculations run in your browser and nothing you type is sent to us.
